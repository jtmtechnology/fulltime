Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

FullTime is a free app for betting against your friends and family on football — no real money ever changes hands, it's just for bragging rights. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Country (optional) — used only to show amounts in your local currency symbol.
  • League and betting activity — the leagues you join, bets you place, and your virtual balance. This is all play-money; no real payment details are ever collected.
  • Push notification token and device time zone offset (Android/iOS app only) — used to send you notifications like bet results and Daily Spinner reminders at roughly the right local time.

Third-party services

We use a small number of third-party services to run the app:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivers push notifications.
  • Google AdMob — shows the occasional interstitial ad in the mobile app, which may collect device/advertising identifiers under Google's own privacy policy.
  • A football data provider — supplies match fixtures, scores and odds. No personal information is shared with this provider.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone beyond what's needed to run the app via the services listed above.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing fulltime@jtmtechnology.co.uk. We'll action it promptly.

Children's privacy

FullTime isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under that age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy? Email fulltime@jtmtechnology.co.uk.
