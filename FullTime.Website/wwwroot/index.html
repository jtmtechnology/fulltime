What's in it

Private leagues

Create a league and share the invite code, or join one with a code from a mate. Everyone starts level, and it's just for the two of you or the whole family — up to 5 leagues at once.

Bet builder

Combine match winner, both teams to score, over/under, correct score and more into your own multi-leg accas across the English pyramid, UEFA competitions and beyond.

Live scores

Follow every match as it happens, with bets settling automatically the moment full time is blown — no chasing anyone to pay up.

Leaderboards

Track your profit in every league you're in, and see how you stack up against everyone else playing FullTime on the worldwide table.

Weekly top-up

Everyone gets a top-up to their balance every week, so the game never runs dry even after a rough run of results.

Totally free

No real money ever changes hands, no gambling licence needed on your end — it's just for the bragging rights.
